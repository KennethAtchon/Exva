# 🧠 The Core Idea (Your “Edge”)

You’re not trying to be right often—you’re trying to be **very right sometimes**.

So your formula is:

> **Asymmetric bets = (Low expectation) + (High potential upside) + (Some real signal you're not crazy)**

---

# 🔍 Step 1: Find Candidates (Deal Flow)

You need a *pipeline* of stocks. Don’t randomly Google.

## 1. Trend Hunting (Momentum + Hype)

Use:

- Reddit (r/wallstreetbets, r/stocks)
- X (Twitter) (finance Twitter)
- Yahoo Finance trending tickers
- Finviz (top gainers, unusual volume)

👉 You’re looking for:

- Sudden spikes in mentions
- Volume spikes
- “Narrative forming”

**Why this matters:**  
Momentum creates short-term inefficiencies → perfect for risky plays.

---

## 2. Undervalued / Ignored Stocks (Hidden Gems)

Use:

- Finviz filters
- Seeking Alpha
- Morningstar

Screen for:

- Small/mid cap
- Low P/E vs industry
- Revenue growth > 15%
- Positive cash flow (optional but strong signal)

👉 You want:

> “Good company, no hype yet”

---

## 3. Special Situations (This is where real money is made)

Look for:

- Earnings coming soon
- Mergers / acquisitions
- New product launches
- Turnarounds (company was trash, now improving)

Follow:

- Earnings calendars
- News on Bloomberg or CNBC

👉 These create **mispricing windows**

---

# 🧪 Step 2: Validate the Idea (Don’t Skip This)

Now you filter the garbage.

## 1. Quick Fundamentals Check (5 min max)

You are NOT doing deep value investing.

Just check:

- Revenue trend (growing or dying?)
- Profitability (or at least improving)
- Debt (not insane)

If it fails here → **skip immediately**

---

## 2. Narrative Check (VERY IMPORTANT)

Ask:

- Why would this stock go up?
- Who is going to buy it after me?

Examples:

- “AI company” → hype buyers
- “Turnaround story” → institutional buyers
- “Undervalued” → slow grind up

If you can’t explain the story in 1 sentence → **you don’t understand it**

---

## 3. Timing Check

This is where most people fail.

You need a **catalyst**:

- Earnings next week
- Product launch
- Viral attention spike

No catalyst = dead money

---

# 📊 Step 3: Entry Strategy (Don’t YOLO Blindly)

## Use simple price logic:

- Buy after breakout (momentum)
- Or buy after pullback (safer)

Avoid:

- Buying at peak hype spike (you’ll get dumped on)

---

# 💣 Step 4: Risk Management (THIS is your real edge)

You said “risky”—fine, but controlled risk.

Rule set:

- Never put >10–20% in one play
- Assume you can lose 100%
- Have exit plan BEFORE entering

Ask yourself:

> “At what point am I wrong?”

---

# 🧮 Your Repeatable “Formula”

Here’s your exact pipeline:

```
1. Find stock (trend / undervalued / special situation)
2. Check fundamentals (quick sanity check)
3. Identify narrative (why will it go up?)
4. Confirm catalyst (why now?)
5. Time entry (don’t chase blindly)
6. Manage risk (position size + exit)

```
